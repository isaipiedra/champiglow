Agaricus bisporus, also known as the Cultivated mushroom, White buttons, Cremini, or the Portobello mushroom. This is the first mushroom you’ve most likely ever eaten. These are the most commonly cultivated and eaten mushrooms around the world. When in their pin forms, they are white and are the white button mushrooms. As they mature they turn brown. When they’re fully brown but still immature, they are Cremini mushrooms. Finally, when they’re fully mature with the cap fully opened, they’re considered Portobello mushrooms. Without these mushrooms, culinary mushroom industries around the world would collapse.

These mushrooms are found all around the world, commonly found in rich soil, compost, or manure. Since these mushrooms are commonly found in the soil, they can also be commonly found growing in fairy rings. Common lookalikes of these can be Amanita species, including the Death Cap or Destroying Angel (Amanita phalloides). The Death Cap mushroom is one of the deadliest mushrooms in the world.

Never consume mushrooms you aren’t 100% sure of! This quick writeup is meant to be informative. If you wish to find and consume mushrooms from the wild, ensure you have the correct mushroom. I am not responsible if you attempt to eat a mushroom that looks like a mushroom from this pack, as there can be potentially many different poisonous lookalikes. If you wish to do your own mushroom hunting, check out some mushroom guide books local to your area (ie A Field Guide To Edible Mushrooms Of The Pacific Northwest if you live in the PNW). You can also try to find local guides who do mushroom hunts where you can learn more about mushrooms in your area.
