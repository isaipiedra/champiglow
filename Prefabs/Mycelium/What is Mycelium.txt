Mycelium can be likened to the roots of the mushroom, but that is not what it actually is. The mushroom body is only the reproduction parts of the entire organism. The mycelium is the actual body of the organism, and is expanding throughout its substrate, absorbing nutrients it needs to produce its fruiting bodies (mushrooms). All mushrooms are actually entirely made up of mycelium as well. If you rip off some of the bark of a rotting tree, you might see white mushroom mycelium growing in a root-like fashion underneath. If you grow mushrooms, you will see mycelium growing in blocks filled with nutrient rich organic matter like straw or manure. I attempted to create seamless mycelium textures and materials using pictures of real mycelium.

Never consume mushrooms you aren’t 100% sure of! This quick writeup is meant to be informative. If you wish to find and consume mushrooms from the wild, ensure you have the correct mushroom. I am not responsible if you attempt to eat a mushroom that looks like a mushroom from this pack, as there can be potentially many different poisonous lookalikes. If you wish to do your own mushroom hunting, check out some mushroom guide books local to your area (ie A Field Guide To Edible Mushrooms Of The Pacific Northwest if you live in the PNW). You can also try to find local guides who do mushroom hunts where you can learn more about mushrooms in your area.
